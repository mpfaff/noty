# Privacy Policy

**Noty**
**Last updated: June 17, 2026**

## Overview

Noty ("the App") is a note-taking app that pins your notes as persistent
notifications. This privacy policy explains what data the App handles and how.

## Data Collection and Usage

### Everything stays on your device

All of your notes are stored **locally on your device** in a private database. The
App has **no network access** and does not transmit any data to any server or third
party. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no backups to external services.

### Permissions and why they are requested

| Permission | Why it is requested |
|---|---|
| **Post Notifications** (Android 13+) | To display your notes as notifications in the status bar — the core function of the App. |
| **Receive Boot Completed** | To restore your pinned notifications after the device restarts. |
| **Foreground Service** / **Foreground Service Data Sync** | To keep the lightweight background service running so your notifications stay active. |

The App requests **no** sensitive permissions — no location, camera, microphone,
contacts, storage, or internet access.

### Data the App does NOT collect

- The App does **not** collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.).
- The App does **not** use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- The App does **not** have internet access and cannot transmit data off your device.
- The App does **not** access your location, contacts, or files.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that collect data.

## Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing
the App's data or uninstalling it. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all notes.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. The
App does not collect personal information from any user.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by
the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

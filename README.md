# Noty - Keep your notes where you'll see them

**Noty** is a simple and effective Android application designed to help you stay organized by pinning your notes directly into your notification bar. Never forget a task or a thought again!

## 🚀 Overview

How many times have you written a note only to forget to check it? Noty solves this by placing your important notes exactly where you look dozens of times a day: your notifications. It’s lightweight, distraction-free, and stays out of your way until you need it.

## ✨ Key Features

-   **📌 Never Forget**: Pin your notes as persistent notifications that stay in your status bar until you're done with them.
-   **🔄 Always There**: Your notifications are automatically restored even after you restart your phone.
-   **🎨 Modern Design**: Beautiful, clean interface built with Material 3, supporting both Light and Dark modes.
-   **⚡ Simple & Fast**: Add, edit, or remove notes with just a few taps. No complex menus, just notes.
-   **🧹 Stay Organized**: Easily manage multiple notes at once.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

### Installation

1.  Clone this repository.
2.  Open the project in **Android Studio**.
3.  Build and run the app on your device or emulator.

### Build Commands

For developers wanting to build from the command line:
-   **Debug APK**: `./gradlew assembleDebug`
-   **Release APK**: `./gradlew assembleRelease`

## 🛡️ Permissions

To provide the best experience, Noty requires a few permissions:
-   **Notifications**: To pin your notes where you can see them.
-   **Run at Startup**: To ensure your notes are restored automatically after your phone restarts.

Noty has **no internet access** and collects no data. See [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
